The reason we're here

The internet needs more third places.

A third place is somewhere beyond home and work where people can gather, follow their curiosity, and feel part of something. storykōbō is being imagined as that kind of place for creative life online.

01 / THE LIBRARY

Discover and learn

Browse creative work, find useful references, and follow a thread of curiosity without needing to keep up with a feed.

02 / THE WORKSHOP

Make and experiment

Give projects room to grow, collect their progress, and share work in whatever state it’s in.

03 / THE COMMONS

Gather and connect

Talk with other creative people, join groups, and take part in community without competing for attention.

What we're building

Tools for the work. Room for the person.

storykōbō is an independent creative community platform for making, sharing, discovering, and connecting. Writing is an important starting point, but creative life is bigger than one medium.

The long-term vision includes creative projects, groups, discussion, events, discovery, and community history — built around a person's own identity, not scattered across disconnected accounts.
