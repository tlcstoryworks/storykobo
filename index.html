What kind of place is this?

Community around the work.

storykōbō isn't where you publish your creations. It's where you meet the people who understand why you make them.

Your work stays yours

We host the community. Not your creations.

Your stories, art, music, games, and other creative works stay wherever you choose to keep them.

storykōbō will not host, publish, store, or serve members' actual creative works. Project pages are community spaces: they help people talk about a project, follow its progress, find collaborators, and visit the external places where the work lives.

A little friendly, competition-free spirit

We're on the same side.

Teams have their own identities, but they're here to bring people together — never to turn creativity into a contest.

TEAMS

Belong together

Event teams have names, symbols, colors, and their own shared spaces. One team per member, per event.

CHEERS

Lift each other up

Cheers are encouragement, not points. Celebrate a return, a breakthrough, a tiny step, or a big finish.

MILESTONES

Keep the good things

Personal awards, event banners, community pennants, and a curated Milestone Gallery preserve what we've done together.

Room for real life

Make things. Encourage someone. Take a break.

Quiet browsing is participation. So is a 140-character update, a thoughtful forum reply, joining a group, or cheering for someone else's progress. You don't have to post every day or prove that you've earned your place.

We want a community that makes room for different bodies, brains, schedules, energy levels, and creative practices.
