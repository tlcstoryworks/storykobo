NOTES FROM THE WORKBENCH

The work is happening in the open.

storykōbō is early in development. The public roadmap and repository are the best places to follow what is changing.

What changed in this direction

The product vision has become more clearly community-centered. Project pages are community objects with descriptions, updates, discussion, collaborators, and external links — never containers for creative works.

We've also clarified the role of groups and event teams, the April and November flagship seasons, flexible progress sharing, and a recognition system built around personal awards, event banners, community pennants, and a curated Milestone Gallery.

Where things stand

  • Established: project foundations, repository, documentation, terminology, and initial Laravel application.
  • Current focus: identity, accounts, usernames, and profiles.
  • Planned: community spaces, project pages, conversation, events, recognition, and operations.
