The workshop handbook

Policies & community standards.

These documents explain how storykōbō is intended to work, how members can participate, and what responsibilities the platform takes on.

These are working drafts. Policies may be revised before launch and as the service develops. Effective dates will be added when policies are formally adopted. A policy's presence here does not mean every described feature or process is already available.

How policy updates work

Policies and website information may change as development progresses, as features are implemented, and as we learn what the community needs. Material changes will be reflected in the relevant documents and communicated through the project's public updates. Published policies will show their version, effective date, and last-updated information.

Before launch, the policy set will be reviewed together and checked against the actual service. Legal, privacy, security, accessibility, and operational details may require revision before adoption.
