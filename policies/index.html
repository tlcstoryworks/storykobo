THE WORKSHOP HANDBOOK

Clear boundaries make better community.

These working documents explain the values and responsibilities that should shape storykōbō. They are drafts for a platform in development, not a claim that every process is already implemented.

Important: storykōbō will not host members' actual creative works. Project pages and community spaces are for descriptions, discussion, progress notes, and external links. See the External Work, Copyright & Linking Policy.

Policy library

Working drafts · Version and effective dates will be finalized before launch.

  • Community Principles
    Belonging, care, accessibility, and voluntary participation.
  • Community Guidelines
    How members treat one another and use shared spaces.
  • External Work, Copyright & Linking
    What project pages can contain, what stays elsewhere, and how links and rights work.
  • Events & Challenges
    Flexible participation, team identity, encouragement, and noncompetitive recognition.
  • Accessibility
    Access needs, flexible participation, and interface expectations.
  • Privacy
    Personal information, visibility, and data practices.
  • Safety, Blocking & Moderation
    Reports, boundaries, moderation, and appeals.
  • Terms of Service
    The service relationship and member responsibilities.

This public policy library is being aligned with the product direction. Formal legal, privacy, security, accessibility, and operational review is still required before adoption.
