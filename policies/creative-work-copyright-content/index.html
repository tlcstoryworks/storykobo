POLICY LIBRARY

External Work, Copyright & Linking

A clear statement of what storykōbō is for — and what it will never host. Working draft · Pre-launch legal and operational review required.

Core boundary: storykōbō will not host, publish, store, or serve members' actual creative works. This is a permanent product boundary, not a temporary technical limitation.

1. Purpose

This policy explains how creative projects may be represented in the community, how members can link to work hosted elsewhere, and how ownership, attribution, permissions, and external content are treated.

2. What storykōbō is — and is not

storykōbō is a community platform. It provides profiles, short social posts, forums, groups, project spaces, events, and community features that help creative people connect.

It is not a publishing platform, manuscript repository, portfolio host, art gallery, audio or video host, game distribution service, or cloud storage service. Members may not upload or submit actual creative works for hosting or display on storykōbō. This includes manuscripts, chapters, stories, poems, illustrations, photographs as creative portfolio submissions, comics, music, recordings, videos, game builds, project files, and comparable works, whether complete or in progress.

3. What project pages may contain

Project pages are community-facing records, not containers for the work itself. They may include:

  • A project title, description, discipline, genre, and status.
  • Creator and collaborator names, where those people agree to be listed.
  • Short progress notes and ordinary community discussion.
  • Links to external websites or services where the creator chooses to host, publish, sell, or present the work.
  • Relevant content notes and descriptive tags for community discovery.

Project pages must not embed, mirror, proxy, or otherwise display the underlying creative work. External links should take visitors to the creator's chosen destination.

4. Community conversation is not work hosting

Members may use short posts, forum discussions, project updates, and comments for ordinary conversation about creative practice and projects. These features are not intended for serially publishing a work, reproducing substantial portions of a work, or using the platform as a substitute for an external publishing or portfolio service. Short social posts are limited to 140 characters; longer discussion belongs in the relevant community spaces.

5. Ownership, permissions, and attribution

Members retain ownership of their original creative works. Providing a link to an external work does not transfer ownership or grant storykōbō a license to that work. Members are responsible for ensuring they have permission to share links, names, descriptions, and any other material they submit to community features.

Credit and permission are different. Giving attribution does not create permission to copy, adapt, reproduce, publish, or distribute another person's work. Do not submit another person's work or private material without appropriate authorization.

6. External services and links

External destinations are operated by third parties. storykōbō does not control their availability, accessibility, privacy practices, moderation, terms, or content. Members should review the terms and privacy information of services they choose to use. A link does not mean storykōbō endorses or verifies the destination.

7. Reports and community concerns

Members may report links, descriptions, or community posts that appear to violate platform policies, infringe rights, expose private information, or create a safety concern. Reports should identify the relevant page or post and explain the concern. The platform may restrict or remove community material or links in accordance with its moderation and legal processes.

8. No general reuse or AI-training license

Submitting ordinary profile information, project descriptions, links, or community posts does not grant permission for unrelated commercial reuse or AI model training. Any limited permissions needed to operate the community features will be described in the final Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. No such permission extends to the underlying creative works hosted elsewhere.

9. Before launch

This working draft must be reconciled with the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, Community Guidelines, and actual technical capabilities. Reporting routes, retention practices, and final legal language require review before adoption.
