POLICY LIBRARY

Community Guidelines

A friendly community doesn't happen by accident. These are the everyday expectations we want to build around.

Treat people like people

Be respectful, even when you disagree. Do not harass, threaten, intimidate, demean, or repeatedly target another member. Respect requests to stop a conversation or leave someone alone.

Ask before critique

Check feedback preferences before offering criticism. If someone asks for encouragement, questions, or no critique, respect that. A project page or public post is not automatic permission to critique or contact someone in any way.

Encourage without keeping score

Cheers are for encouragement, not points. Do not use team identity, awards, milestones, or progress updates to belittle, rank, pressure, or shame other members. There are no winners and losers in belonging.

Respect different ways to participate

Members may share often, browse quietly, join a conversation occasionally, take breaks, or return after a long absence. Do not demand explanations for someone's pace, health, disability, caregiving, or availability.

Keep creative work where its creator chooses

storykōbō does not host actual creative works. Do not attempt to use profiles, project pages, posts, comments, or forums to publish or reproduce creative works. Project spaces may contain descriptions, ordinary progress notes, conversation, and links to external destinations. See the External Work, Copyright & Linking Policy.

Respect authorship and permission

Do not claim another person's work as yours, misrepresent collaboration, or share private material without permission. Do not submit another member's creative work to an AI system or external processing service without their explicit permission.

Use community spaces in good faith

Do not spam, impersonate people to deceive or harm them, manipulate community systems, misuse reporting, or retaliate against someone for setting a boundary or making a good-faith report.

Respect privacy and safety

Do not publish another member's private information, stalk or monitor them, or try to access accounts or spaces you are not allowed to use. Do not attempt to identify, locate, or contact someone outside the platform in ways they have not welcomed.

Follow event and group expectations

Groups and events may have additional rules. Those rules cannot override platform-wide policies or the expectation that participation remains voluntary, flexible, and noncompetitive.

Working status

These guidelines are a working draft for a platform in development. Reporting, blocking, moderation, and appeal procedures must be implemented and tested before launch.
