PLAIN-LANGUAGE COMMUNITY GUIDE

Be kind. Be curious. Make room.

We want a place where people can create, connect, and feel welcome without having to compete for attention.

People first

Disagree without attacking. Respect boundaries. Ask before giving critique, and don't pressure someone to respond or share more than they want.

Cheer, don't score

Cheers mean you're rooting for someone. They aren't points. Teams, awards, and milestones are for belonging and celebration, not ranking people.

Different paces belong

You can post every day, check in once a month, browse quietly, or take a break. You don't have to explain illness, disability, caregiving, or absence.

Your work stays elsewhere

storykōbō will never host actual creative works. Project pages are for descriptions, progress notes, conversation, and links to external places where the work lives.

Respect privacy and permission

Don't share private information or someone else's work without permission. Respect blocking, report genuine concerns, and don't retaliate against people who set boundaries.
