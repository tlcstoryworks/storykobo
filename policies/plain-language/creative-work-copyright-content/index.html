The formal Creative Work, Copyright & Content Policy is the governing document.

Your work belongs to you

You keep ownership of the original work you create. When you post something, you give storykōbō only the permission it needs to host and display what you chose to share. That is not permission to sell it, publish it elsewhere, reuse it for unrelated purposes, or train AI models on it.

Credit and permission are different. Crediting someone does not give you the right to copy or adapt their work.

What belongs on storykōbō

The platform is for excerpts, project updates, discussion, and creative community activity—not full manuscripts. You can link to an outside place where you host the complete work.

Only share collaborative or commissioned material within the permission you actually have. Private agreements about collaborations, commissions, or publishing are between the members involved.

Labels and content choices

We plan to use ordinary tags, required content warnings, maturity-triggering tags, and prohibited-content rules. Members will be able to opt into content and use include/exclude preferences. Project defaults and personal settings can help tailor what you see, but they cannot override platform rules.

If you have not opted into a type of restricted content, the planned experience is a neutral placeholder with a safe title—not an excerpt or thumbnail that gives the content away.

The exact categories and controls still need to be finalized and built before launch.

Difficult themes and fiction

Fiction can depict difficult subjects without endorsing them. We want room for creative exploration while still setting clear boundaries. Original fiction is the community's outreach focus, but fan works are not automatically excluded. Context and the same content rules apply to both.

Copyright reports and removal

If you believe someone has shared your work without permission, there should be a confidential way to report it. The platform may temporarily restrict disputed material while reviewing the claim. A report is not automatically proof, and affected members should have a chance to respond.

You can request removal of work you own or control. Some copies may need to be retained temporarily for legal, safety, security, or backup reasons. Removing a storykōbō copy cannot remove copies hosted elsewhere or independently saved by other people.

Repeated or deliberate infringement may lead to stronger moderation action than a good-faith mistake.

Before launch

We still need exact excerpt limits, content categories, reporting instructions, response timelines, and working removal controls. The published policy must describe what the site can actually do.
