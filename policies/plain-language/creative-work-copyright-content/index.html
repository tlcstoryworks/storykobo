PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY GUIDE

Your work stays yours.

The short version: storykōbō is for the community around creative work. The work itself lives somewhere else, wherever you choose.

We will never host your actual creative work. No manuscript uploads, art portfolio uploads, music or video hosting, game files, or publishing tools. This is intentional and permanent.

What can I put on a project page?

You can describe your project, choose its discipline and genre, list collaborators, share its status, post short progress notes, talk with people, and link to the place where the work lives.

Where does the actual work go?

Wherever you want it to go. Your own website, portfolio, publishing platform, gallery, store, audio service, game page, or another external home. You decide where it lives and what terms apply there.

Does storykōbō own my work?

No. You keep ownership of what you create. Linking to it does not give storykōbō ownership or permission to copy it, publish it, sell it, or train AI models on it.

What about posts and discussions?

Community features are for conversation: short updates, questions, project notes, and discussion. They are not a workaround for serially publishing a story, uploading an illustration, or reproducing a creative work in pieces.

Why make this distinction?

Because we want to build a community, not another platform trying to become the home for everything. Your creative work should stay under your control. storykōbō is the place to find people who care about it.
