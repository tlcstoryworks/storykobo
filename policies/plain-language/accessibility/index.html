The formal Accessibility Policy is the governing document.

Accessibility belongs here

People should not have to be healthy, energetic, fast, or able-bodied to belong in storykōbō. Accessibility is part of how the platform and community are meant to work.

What we're working toward

The website should work with keyboards and assistive technology, use clear labels and headings, have readable text and sufficient contrast, support zoom and different screen sizes, and respect reduced-motion settings. Important information should not be available only through an image, sound, or other inaccessible format.

We need both automated checks and hands-on testing. We will not claim that the site is fully accessible without evidence.

Community participation

Do not shame someone for disability, illness, neurodivergence, caregiving, low energy, assistive technology, or participating at a different pace. Challenges and events should not make speed, constant availability, word counts, or public performance the price of belonging.

You do not have to disclose a diagnosis or provide medical proof to ask for a participation adjustment.

Asking for help or reporting a barrier

There should be a clear way to tell us when something is inaccessible or ask for an adjustment. We should respond respectfully, explain what is possible, and consider alternatives without asking for unnecessary personal medical information.

Events and ongoing work

Event organizers should think about time zones, sensory needs, energy, communication styles, and different ways to participate. Accessibility is never finished; we will keep learning and improving, and be honest about known limitations.

Before launch, core journeys and the accessibility feedback route must be tested and working.
