PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY GUIDE

Events are invitations, not obligations.

Make something with us. Cheer someone on. Take a break when you need one. All of those things belong here.

Teams are for togetherness

Each event has its own teams. You belong to one team for that event, and you might be on a different team next time. Teams have names, symbols, colors, and shared spaces. They are not rivals, and there are no losing teams.

Goals are yours to choose

You might count words, scenes, chapters, minutes, sessions, revision passes, or something that makes sense for your creative practice. You can also participate without a number. November is for drafting; April is for revision. Neither has one required goal.

Progress is optional to share

You can keep progress private, share it with your team, or share it with the community. A plain-language update can matter just as much as a number. Nobody should be ranked or shamed for their pace.

Recognition is for celebrating

Personal awards recognize milestones. Event banners keep a little memory of the event, with your team's symbol, name, and year on a side ribbon and pixel-art keepsakes across the banner. Community pennants celebrate things accomplished together.

There's no leaderboard

The Milestone Gallery is a curated exhibition, not a ranking. It's a place to browse what people have achieved and celebrate community history.

Take part in the way that works

Illness, disability, caregiving, changing schedules, and limited energy are normal parts of life. You don't owe anyone an explanation for changing your goal, missing a day, or stepping away.
