ONE PIECE AT A TIME

A map of the work ahead.

This roadmap describes the direction of development, not a delivery calendar. Planned concepts are not live features, and priorities may change as the project grows.

Current public status: the repository, core documentation, initial Laravel application, and local development foundation are established. Username validation and uniqueness are implemented and tested. The full account and profile experience remains in development.

Phase 0 — Foundation

Established
  • Repository and project documentation
  • Working terminology and architecture principles
  • Initial Laravel application and local environment

Phase 1 — Identity

Current focus
  • Account registration and authentication
  • Permanent numeric user IDs and username claiming
  • Public profiles, account settings, and privacy foundations
  • Username validation and uniqueness — implemented and tested

Phase 2 — Community foundation

Planned
  • Roles, groups, memberships, and group discovery
  • Following and community activity
  • Short posts (140 characters), threads, and notifications

Phase 3 — Projects & connections

Planned
  • Community-facing project pages and project updates
  • Creative disciplines, genres, collaborators, and status
  • External links to creators' chosen homes for their work
  • No creative-work hosting, publishing, or portfolio uploads

Phase 4 — Conversation & community

Planned
  • Forum categories, threads, replies, and moderation tools
  • Cheers and encouragement
  • Project and group discussion spaces

Phase 5 — Recognition & history

Planned
  • Personal awards, visible earned and locked award icons
  • Personal event banners with team side ribbons and pixel-art keepsakes
  • Community pennants and curated Milestone Gallery
  • Participation and community history archives

Phase 6 — Events & teams

Planned
  • April revision and November drafting seasons
  • Flexible goals, optional progress updates, and visibility choices
  • One team per participant per event, with team identity and gathering spaces
  • Smaller events and community challenges between flagship seasons

Phase 7 — Operations & trust

Planned
  • Moderation, reporting, blocking, and administrative tools
  • Email, backups, logging, data export, and deployment practices
  • Policy and accessibility checks against actual platform behavior

Phase 8 — Integrations & sustainability

Longer term
  • Useful integrations and public APIs where justified
  • Optional supporter funding and contribution tracking
  • Thoughtful ways to sustain the project without compromising the community

What this roadmap is — and isn't

Features in future phases are plans and ideas, not currently available functionality or dated commitments. We will keep the roadmap honest as technical findings, resources, and community needs shape the work.
