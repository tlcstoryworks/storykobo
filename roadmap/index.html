ONE PIECE AT A TIME

A map of the work ahead.

This roadmap describes the direction of development, not a delivery calendar. Planned concepts are not live features, and priorities may change as the project grows.

Current project status: the public site, repository, core documentation, initial Laravel application, and local development foundation are established. Username validation and uniqueness are implemented and tested. Backend development is taking a planning pause during November while we define launch scope, engineering standards, and the work needed for a sustainable first release.

Foundations for the long haul

These standards apply across every phase. They are part of building the platform, not a cleanup task to postpone until later.

Maintainable engineering

Project-wide standard
  • Readable, consistently organized code with useful comments and documentation close to the implementation
  • Clear boundaries between presentation, application logic, data, and permissions
  • Documented system relationships, dependencies, and architectural decisions
  • Automated tests for important behavior, permissions, and regressions
  • Database changes managed through versioned migrations; secrets kept out of source control
  • Deliberate dependency updates and a documented framework maintenance approach

Accessibility, safety & reliability

Project-wide standard
  • Accessible interaction patterns and reduced-motion support considered during implementation
  • Privacy, security, moderation, and reporting designed into the relevant systems
  • Backups, tested recovery procedures, error logging, and deployment documentation
  • Administrative tools for routine community care without direct database edits
  • Systems designed to evolve without requiring a complete rebuild or sacrificing a familiar member experience

Development phases

Phase 0 — Foundation

Established
  • Repository and project documentation
  • Working terminology and architecture principles
  • Initial Laravel application and local environment

Phase 1 — Identity

Next backend focus
  • Account registration and authentication
  • Permanent numeric user IDs and username claiming
  • Public profiles, account settings, and privacy foundations
  • Username validation and uniqueness — implemented and tested

Phase 2 — Community foundation

Planned
  • Roles, groups, memberships, and group discovery
  • Following and community activity
  • Short posts (140 characters), threads, and notifications

Phase 3 — Projects & connections

Planned
  • Community-facing project pages and project updates
  • Creative disciplines, genres, collaborators, and status
  • External links to creators' chosen homes for their work
  • No creative-work hosting, publishing, or portfolio uploads

Phase 4 — Conversation & community

Planned
  • Forum categories, threads, replies, and moderation tools
  • Cheers and encouragement
  • Project and group discussion spaces

Phase 5 — Recognition & history

Planned
  • Personal awards, visible earned and locked award icons
  • Personal event banners with team side ribbons and pixel-art keepsakes
  • Community pennants and curated Milestone Gallery
  • Participation and community history archives

Phase 6 — Events & teams

Planned
  • April revision and November drafting seasons
  • Flexible goals, optional progress updates, and visibility choices
  • One team per participant per event, with team identity and gathering spaces
  • Smaller events and community challenges between flagship seasons

Phase 7 — Operations & trust

Planned
  • Moderation, reporting, blocking, and administrative tools
  • Email, backups, logging, data export, and deployment practices
  • Policy and accessibility checks against actual platform behavior
  • Operational documentation, recovery drills, and maintenance routines

Phase 8 — Integrations & sustainability

Longer term
  • Useful integrations and public APIs where justified
  • Optional supporter funding and contribution tracking
  • Thoughtful ways to sustain the project without compromising the community

What this roadmap is — and isn't

Features in future phases are plans and ideas, not currently available functionality or dated commitments. The phases describe product areas, not a promise that each must be completed in strict sequence. Security, accessibility, testing, documentation, and maintainability are ongoing responsibilities throughout development.

We will keep the roadmap honest as technical findings, resources, and community needs shape the work. Meaningful changes and decisions will be recorded in the project updates.
