Get to know storykōbō.

An independent creative community platform in the making — built around the belief that making things is better when we have room to learn, share, and connect.

What is storykōbō?

storykōbō is a long-term project to bring creative people and creative work together in one welcoming place. It takes inspiration from the library, the workshop, the archive, and the community commons: places where people can follow their interests, work at their own pace, and encounter one another without everything becoming a competition.

Today, creative life is spread across social networks, forums, chat servers, event sites, and personal project pages. storykōbō imagines a more connected home for that life — one where a person's identity and creative work can belong together.

Who is it for?

The intended community is for adults aged 18 and older. Writing is an important starting point, but it is not the limit. Fiction, poetry, worldbuilding, comics, game writing, zines, nonfiction, visual storytelling, collaborative work, and other creative practices all belong in the picture.

Where are we now?

The project is in its foundation and architecture phase. The initial application foundation and project documentation are in place, and the first major product milestone is identity: accounts, permanent user IDs, username claiming, and public profiles. The larger platform will grow in deliberate stages.

This is a project in progress. The pages here describe the direction and current plans, not a finished platform. We’ll keep the roadmap honest as the work changes.

Who is making it?

storykōbō is an independent project by tlc storyworks. Its visual identity and community are being developed with care, with a focus on accessibility, creative freedom, and a place to belong without pressure to perform.
