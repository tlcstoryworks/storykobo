A place for the people behind the projects.

storykōbō is an independent, adult creative community in the making. A place to find your people, talk about what you're making, and make room for one another.

So, what is storykōbō?

Think community spaces, forums, short social updates, project pages, groups, and event teams — with a little of the warmth and personality of the old indie web. It's a place to gather around creative life, not a platform that expects every part of that life to happen on-site.

The boundary is deliberate: storykōbō will never host members' actual creative works. We won't be a manuscript host, art gallery, music player, video host, game-file host, publishing service, or portfolio replacement. Your work stays on the services and spaces you choose. We provide the community around it.

What a project page is for

A project page is a community-facing home for context and connection. It can describe a project, list its creative discipline and genre, introduce collaborators, show its status, share short progress updates, and link out to the work wherever it is hosted.

It is not a place to upload the novel, illustration, song, video, game build, or other finished or in-progress creative work itself.

Who is it for?

Adults aged 18 and older, across creative practices: fiction, poetry, worldbuilding, visual art, comics, zines, game development, music, crafts, nonfiction, roleplaying, collaborative projects, and things that don't fit neatly into a category.

What makes it different?

  • People before metrics. No popularity ladder, public productivity ranking, or engagement treadmill.
  • Encouragement over rivalry. Teams bring people together for events; they don't compete against one another.
  • Participation has many shapes. Share, discuss, browse quietly, take a break, or return when life allows.
  • Your work remains yours. External links point to your chosen home for the actual creation.
