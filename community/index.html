How we hope to be together

A community with room for different ways to belong.

A creative community should make space for people who share every day, people who join a conversation now and then, and people who mostly prefer to browse quietly.

Adults, by design

storykōbō is intended as an 18+ community. The goal is to design for adult participation from the beginning rather than retrofit an adult-only environment onto a general-audience platform.

Many kinds of creative life

People create in different ways and for different reasons. The community should be able to make room for writing, art, worldbuilding, comics, zines, game writing, collaborative projects, and interests that don't fit neatly into a single category.

Participation without a scoreboard

We want to encourage people without making public popularity the measure of their value. Recognition, achievements, event participation, and community history can be meaningful without becoming leaderboards or productivity contests.

More than one way to take part

Browse quietly

Reading, discovering, and following work should be valid ways to spend time in the community.

Share and make

People should be able to present projects, share progress, and decide what they are ready to make public.

Connect at your own pace

Groups, conversations, and events should offer invitations to participate, not pressure to be constantly visible.

Privacy matters

Discovery should not require people to reveal information they have chosen to keep private. Contextual groups and personal details may need stronger visibility controls.

Accessibility and customization

The interface should not assume one aesthetic, pace, or participation style works for everyone. Accessibility and personal choice are part of the foundation, not decorative extras.

Still being shaped

These are working principles for a project in development, not a complete community rulebook. Specific moderation policies, reporting processes, and community operations will need to be developed before the platform opens.

A simple promise

No one right way to be here.

Make something. Encourage someone. Join an event. Read quietly. Take a break. A community can be alive without asking everyone to show up in the same way.
