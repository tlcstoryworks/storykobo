HOW WE HOPE TO BE TOGETHER

We're on the same side.

A creative community should make room for people who share every day, people who join now and then, and people who mostly prefer to browse quietly.

A friendly place, on purpose

Kindness is part of the architecture.

We want people to be able to show up as themselves, share what they're comfortable sharing, and trust that encouragement doesn't come with a hidden score.

Assume good faith

Be curious before being critical. Disagree with ideas without demeaning people.

Ask before critique

Feedback is personal. Respect requests for encouragement, specific feedback, or no critique at all.

Respect the pause

No one owes the community constant activity, explanations for absence, or proof of why they need flexibility.

Groups are not teams

Two kinds of belonging.

⌂

Groups are ongoing

Gather around an interest, creative practice, identity, region, or shared context. Groups can continue for as long as their members find value in them.

✳

Teams are event-specific

For an event, each participant belongs to one team. Teams have names, symbols, colors, and shared spaces. A person can be on a different team in the next event.

Teams are here to cheer each other on — not to defeat another team.

Everyday community

Small ways to be here count.

Short posts

Conversational updates are intentionally brief: 140 characters, with threads when a thought needs more room. They're for everyday moments, not long-form publishing.

Forums

Longer conversations have their own spaces, with topics, replies, and room for thoughtful discussion.

Cheers

A cheer means “I'm rooting for you.” It's an expression of encouragement, not a reaction score, currency, or competitive point.

Quiet participation

Reading, discovering, following a project, or taking a break are all valid ways to belong.

Accessibility & care

There is no single right pace.

We don't want events or community systems to assume unlimited time, energy, mobility, attention, or reliable health. Goals should be flexible, progress sharing should be optional, and participation should not require disclosing private medical information.

Our intended community is for adults aged 18 and older. Safety, moderation, privacy, and accessibility policies are being developed alongside the product.
