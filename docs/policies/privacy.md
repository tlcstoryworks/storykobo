# Privacy Policy

**Status:** Approved working draft — pre-launch legal and operational review required  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Effective date:** To be set before launch

> This working draft identifies privacy commitments and decisions that must be mapped to the real application, hosting environment, analytics, email, payment (if any), and other vendors. It is not a verified inventory of current data collection and is not legal advice.

## 1. Our approach

storykōbō is intended to collect and use only information reasonably needed to operate the service, support member choices, protect accounts and community spaces, and meet applicable obligations. Privacy commitments must be reflected in the actual application and vendor configuration, not only in policy language.

## 2. Information that may be handled

Depending on features actually offered, information may include:
- Account details, including username, email address, birthdate or age-eligibility information, and account preferences.
- Profile, project, excerpt, tag, comment, message, event, and other content a member chooses to submit.
- Member settings, including content preferences, accessibility preferences, and interaction or critique preferences.
- Reports, moderation records, appeals, and communications with the service.
- Technical and security information such as login events, device or browser information, IP address, diagnostic data, and security logs.
- Information supplied by a member when requesting support, export, correction, or closure.

The final published policy must list actual categories collected by the live service and must not imply that an unimplemented feature is active.

## 3. Purposes

Information may be used to:
- Create and maintain accounts and provide requested features.
- Display content and apply member-selected visibility, preference, and interaction settings.
- Operate community spaces, events, and communications.
- Respond to reports, appeals, support requests, abuse, and security incidents.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the service.
- Comply with legal obligations and protect rights, safety, and service integrity.

Uses beyond these purposes require an appropriate basis, clear notice, and any consent or choice required by law.

## 4. Creative work and AI training

Members retain ownership of their creative work. storykōbō does not claim a general right to sell, license, or use member work to train AI models. Member work must not be scraped, harvested, or supplied for AI training without the creator's explicit permission. Assistive tools and permitted uses are addressed in the AI Use & Human Authorship Policy.

The platform's own processing needed to host, secure, and display content must be distinguished from external model training or unrelated reuse.

## 5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by vendors that provide necessary hosting, storage, email, security, analytics, or other services, but only as appropriate to operate the service and subject to suitable safeguards and agreements. The final policy must identify relevant provider categories and, where required, specific providers.

We do not sell member personal information to advertisers. Any future advertising, payment, marketplace, or data-sharing model would require a new privacy review and updated notice before introduction.

Information may also be disclosed when required or permitted by applicable law, to respond to valid legal process, or to address urgent safety, security, or rights-protection concerns. Any disclosure should be limited to what is necessary and legally appropriate.

## 6. Visibility and member choice

The service should make visibility and sharing choices understandable. Private account or report information should not be exposed publicly. Reports are anonymous to ordinary members and the reported member, but visible to authorized moderators or staff who need the information to handle the case.

The final policy must explain the actual visibility of profiles, projects, comments, messages, event participation, and other content.

## 7. Security

We use and maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards proportionate to the information and risks involved. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. The internal security program governs access controls, encryption, backups, vulnerability handling, incident response, and recovery.

Members should use unique passwords and notify the service if they suspect account compromise. Security controls must be tested before launch; this policy does not certify that they are already implemented.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, safety and accountability needs, service integrity, and applicable legal obligations. Different categories may require different retention periods.

Account closure and deletion requests are addressed in the Account, Data Export & Closure Policy. Some information may remain temporarily in protected backups or be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or unresolved safety matters. The final policy must explain actual timelines and exceptions rather than promise immediate deletion if systems cannot provide it.

## 9. Member rights and requests

Depending on applicable law and the member's location, members may have rights to access, correct, export, delete, or restrict certain personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. The service will provide a contact route and explain how to submit requests. The final policy must identify the responsible operator and response process.

## 10. Children and age eligibility

storykōbō is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not designed for children. The service's age-eligibility process should collect no more than necessary. The current product direction is to request birthdate without requiring identity documents or legal paperwork. The final approach must be checked for legal compliance and implemented accurately.

## 11. International processing and changes

Vendors or infrastructure may process information in locations other than a member's home jurisdiction. The final policy must identify relevant locations and safeguards where required. Material changes to collection, use, or sharing will be communicated and consent obtained where required.

## 12. Contact and readiness

Before publication, add the actual legal operator, privacy contact, request route, vendor list, jurisdiction-specific notices, retention schedule, and incident-notification process. The plain-language companion is explanatory; this policy governs if they differ. This document is not launch-ready until the real data flows have been audited against it.
