# AI Use & Human Authorship Policy

**Status:** Approved working draft — pre-launch review required  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Effective date:** To be set before launch

## 1. Purpose

storykōbō is a community for human creative work and authorship. This policy distinguishes assistive uses of AI from AI-generated creative media, undisclosed substitution for human authorship, and the use of another member's work or information in external AI systems.

## 2. Human authorship

Members are expected to represent their creative contributions honestly. AI assistance does not automatically erase human authorship, but members must not misrepresent substantially AI-generated work as wholly human-created where that distinction is material to the community, a project, a challenge, or a stated participation rule.

Members remain responsible for what they submit, including accuracy, rights, privacy, and compliance with community policies.

## 3. Assistive tools

Assistive uses may include brainstorming support, outlining, organization, accessibility support, spelling and grammar assistance, research assistance, translation, or other tools that help a person develop and communicate their own work.

The relevant distinction is whether the human remains meaningfully responsible for the creative decisions and final expression, and whether the use is consistent with the rules of the particular space or activity. Members should disclose assistance when a challenge, collaboration, or feature specifically requires it, or when nondisclosure would materially mislead others.

## 4. AI-generated media in creative spaces

AI-generated media is not permitted as creative work or creative-media submissions in storykōbō creative spaces under the current community direction. This includes presenting generated text, images, audio, or other media as a member-created creative submission. The final launch rules should define any narrow exceptions, if any, before features or challenges rely on them.

This restriction does not automatically prohibit ordinary assistive tools that do not generate the submitted creative media, as described above.

## 5. Other members' work and external systems

Members must not submit, upload, paste, or otherwise provide another member's work, private communications, personal information, or unpublished creative material to an AI system or external processing service without that person's explicit permission.

This applies even when the purpose is critique, summarization, editing, translation, training, experimentation, or convenience. Public visibility on storykōbō is not consent for external AI processing.

Members must also respect copyright, privacy, confidentiality, and collaboration permissions when using any external tool.

## 6. Platform use and training

storykōbō does not claim a general right to use member creative work to train AI models. Member work must not be scraped, harvested, or supplied to AI training or datasets without explicit permission from the rights holder. Any future platform feature involving AI processing of member content would require clear advance explanation, an appropriate opt-in, and a privacy and policy review before release.

## 7. Disclosure and community trust

When disclosure is required, it should be factual and proportionate: identify the kind of assistance used and its role without demanding private prompts, drafts, or personal information. Members should not weaponize accusations of AI use to harass, shame, or exclude others. Concerns should be raised through appropriate community processes and assessed using evidence and context.

## 8. Events, challenges, and collaborations

An event or challenge may set additional authorship or tool-use requirements, provided they are clearly stated before participation. Those requirements cannot weaken platform-wide prohibitions on AI-generated creative media or unauthorized processing of another member's work. Collaborators should agree directly on tool use and disclosure for their project.

## 9. Enforcement and related policies

Misrepresentation, unauthorized processing, scraping, or misuse of another member's work may also violate the Acceptable Use Policy, Community Guidelines, Privacy Policy, or Creative Work, Copyright & Content Policy. Related concerns should be coordinated where appropriate rather than duplicating investigations or penalties.

## 10. Readiness and changes

The effective date will be set before launch. Any future change to the platform's AI position must be communicated clearly and must not silently convert member work into training or processing material. The plain-language companion is explanatory; this policy governs if they differ.
