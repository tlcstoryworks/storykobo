# Events & Challenges Policy

**Status:** Approved working draft — pre-launch review required  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Effective date:** To be set before launch

## 1. Purpose

Events and challenges are optional ways to share creative energy and connect. They should support the writer-first, low-pressure character of storykōbō rather than create competition, obligation, or a productivity hierarchy.

## 2. Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. Members may join, pause, change their level of participation, or leave without shame or penalty. No one should be pressured to explain illness, disability, caregiving, limited time, missed goals, or absence.

Events should not use leaderboards, public productivity rankings, or participation streaks as measures of member worth. Any progress tracking should be private or opt-in and should not become a popularity contest.

## 3. Clear event information

Before an event begins, organizers should explain its purpose, dates, time zones, activities, eligibility, participation expectations, any content boundaries, and how to ask questions or raise concerns. If an event includes a challenge, the rules and any judging criteria must be clear before entries are submitted.

## 4. Accessibility and flexible participation

Organizers should consider different energy levels, schedules, time zones, devices, access needs, and communication preferences. Where practical, offer asynchronous participation, flexible pacing, alternative formats, breaks, and ways to participate without public posting.

A challenge should not require members to disclose private health or personal information. Adjustments should be considered respectfully and without unnecessary proof.

## 5. Content, authorship, and conduct

All event activity remains subject to the Community Guidelines, Acceptable Use Policy, Creative Work, Copyright & Content Policy, AI Use & Human Authorship Policy, and maturity/content rules. Event rules may add clear requirements for a particular activity but cannot override platform-wide restrictions.

If an event has authorship, AI-tool, originality, or disclosure requirements, state them before participation. Members remain responsible for having permission to submit work and for respecting other participants' boundaries.

## 6. Feedback, judging, and recognition

Feedback should follow the creator's stated preferences. Critique is not assumed to be welcome merely because work appears in an event. If an event includes judging or awards, criteria, decision-makers, conflicts, and any prizes should be disclosed in advance. Recognition should not imply that non-participants or non-winners are less valued.

## 7. Organizers and moderation

Organizers must apply event rules consistently, avoid favoritism and retaliation, disclose relevant conflicts, and refer safety or policy concerns through the established reporting process. Event organizers do not gain authority to override platform policies or moderation decisions.

## 8. Changes and cancellation

Organizers should communicate material changes, delays, or cancellation as promptly as practical. If an event cannot continue safely or responsibly, it may be paused or ended. Members should not be penalized for changes outside their control.

## 9. Readiness and related policies

Event templates and tools should support clear rules, accessibility information, content boundaries, and voluntary participation. The plain-language companion is explanatory; this policy governs if they differ.
