## Status and publication

These documents are working drafts for the pre-launch policy set. Version numbers and effective dates must be finalized before launch. The existence of a policy does not mean every described feature, safeguard, reporting route, or procedure has been implemented.

Before public publication, review the full set together for consistency, complete operational details, confirm legal and privacy requirements, and ensure every statement matches the live service. Public-facing policy pages should be prepared only after that review.

## Related planning

The internal security program is maintained separately from this member-facing policy library. Funding and transaction terms are deferred until the service introduces payments, paid services, a marketplace, or another transaction feature that requires them.
