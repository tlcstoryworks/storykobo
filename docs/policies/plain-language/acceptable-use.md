# Acceptable Use: The Plain-Language Guide

**Status:** Approved working draft — pre-launch review required  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Effective date:** To be set before launch

This guide explains the Acceptable Use Policy in everyday language. The formal policy is the governing document if anything here differs.

## What this policy is for

The Community Guidelines cover how we treat one another. The Acceptable Use Policy covers serious misuse of the platform: behavior that puts people, their work, accounts, or the service at risk.

## What you may not do

Do not use storykōbō to threaten, stalk, exploit, harass, scam, impersonate, recruit for violent extremism, organize real-world violence, or pressure people into abusive groups. Do not spam, manipulate engagement, or disrupt community spaces.

Do not break into accounts or systems, bypass permissions, exploit security weaknesses, interfere with the service, or collect people's information or writing without permission. That includes scraping work or member data for AI training or datasets.

Do not evade a moderation restriction by switching accounts. Do not invent evidence, knowingly file false reports, coordinate dishonest complaints, or retaliate against someone for reporting or appealing.

## Multiple accounts are allowed

Pen names, pseudonyms, separate creative identities, and multiple accounts are not automatically a problem. What matters is whether someone uses them to deceive, impersonate, evade a restriction, or manipulate the community. Sharing a home, device, or network does not by itself prove wrongdoing.

## Context matters

A story can portray harmful behavior without endorsing it. Historical discussion, criticism, education, journalism, satire, and fiction are not automatically violations. But calling something fiction does not excuse threats, targeted harassment, exploitation, recruitment, or material intended to help someone cause real-world harm.

## Reports and consequences

You can report something you experienced or directly witnessed. A good-faith report is protected even if the moderators cannot confirm it. Knowingly fabricated reports and retaliation are different.

A single incident may touch several policies. We aim to handle connected issues together rather than investigate or punish the same incident repeatedly. Separate incidents may still be handled separately. Responses depend on the situation and may range from a warning to restrictions or account removal. You can find the process in the Safety, Blocking & Moderation Policy.

## What storykōbō does not do

The platform manages participation in its community. It does not act as the broker, lawyer, guarantor, or enforcement service for private deals between members, such as commissions, collaborations, licenses, or publishing agreements. We can still address misconduct that happens through community interactions.

Some reporting tools and safeguards described in the formal policy may not be built yet. They must be ready before launch, and this guide will be updated alongside the formal policy.
