# Privacy: The Plain-Language Guide

**Status:** Approved working draft — pre-launch legal and operational review required  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Effective date:** To be set before launch

The formal Privacy Policy is the governing document. This guide explains the goals in everyday language.

## What we want to collect

storykōbō should collect only what it needs to run the service, provide the features you choose, protect accounts and community spaces, and meet legal responsibilities. Depending on the features in use, that may include account details, things you post, your settings, reports or appeals, support messages, and technical security information.

The published policy must describe what the live site actually collects. This draft is not a claim that every listed feature is already built.

## Your writing and AI training

You keep ownership of your writing. storykōbō does not claim a general right to sell or use member work to train AI models. Other people may not scrape or collect your work for AI training without your explicit permission. The AI policy explains the difference between assistive tools and training or reuse.

## Who may receive information

Service providers may handle information when they are needed to host, store, secure, or operate the service. We may also have to disclose information in response to valid legal requirements or urgent safety concerns. The final policy must name the actual provider categories and explain the real data flows.

We do not sell member personal information to advertisers. A future advertising, payment, or marketplace model would need a new privacy review and updated notice.

## Reports and private information

Reports are not shown to ordinary members or the person reported, but authorized moderators and staff handling the case can see who submitted them. Private account and report information should not be made public.

## Security, retention, and deletion

We plan to use safeguards appropriate to the information we hold, but no online service can promise perfect security. We should keep information only as long as it is needed, while allowing for legal requirements, safety cases, and protected backups.

Closing an account does not necessarily erase every copy instantly. The Account, Data Export & Closure Policy will explain the process and exceptions.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to see, correct, export, or delete certain information. The published policy must provide a real contact route and explain how requests work.

## Adults only

storykōbō is intended for people 18 and older. The current plan is to use birthdate for age eligibility rather than asking for identity documents. The final process must be legally checked and must match the live site.

## Before this is published

We must check the actual application, hosting, vendors, logs, retention periods, and request process. This guide will be updated whenever the formal policy changes.
