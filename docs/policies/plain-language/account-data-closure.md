# Account, Data Export & Closure: The Plain-Language Guide

**Status:** Approved working draft — pre-launch review required  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Effective date:** To be set before launch

The formal Account, Data Export & Closure Policy is the governing document.

## Your account is yours to leave

You should be able to request account closure without being pressured to stay. The service should explain what happens before an irreversible closure is completed.

## Getting a copy of your information

You should have a way to request an export of your account information and your own work. The export should be in a reasonably usable format and explain what it includes.

We cannot provide someone else's private information or confidential moderation records. The actual export format, delivery method, and timing must be tested and published before launch.

## What happens to your posts and projects?

Closing an account and removing every contribution are not necessarily the same thing. We need to explain how your own work, replies, quotations, collaborative projects, and shared discussions are handled.

You keep ownership of your work. We should respect your choices while also considering other people's rights, shared context, legal requirements, and safety concerns. The exact rules must be settled before launch; this guide does not promise that every contribution is always kept or always erased.

## Backups and exceptions

Some information may need to be kept for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or unresolved safety reasons. Deleted information may also remain in protected backups for a limited period. The service should explain the real timelines and should not put deleted information back into active use unnecessarily.

## Confirming a request

We should verify that the person making a request controls the account, but ask for no more information than needed. The process should not demand identity documents or legal paperwork unless a specific legal or security need requires them.

## Before launch

We still need a working request route and tested procedures for exports, closure, content handling, deletion, and backups. The published policy must describe what the system actually does.
