# Acceptable Use Policy

**Status:** Approved working draft — pre-launch review required  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Effective date:** To be set before launch  
**Applies to:** All storykōbō accounts, features, services, and community spaces

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy describes serious misuse of storykōbō and conduct that threatens members, their work, the service, or the integrity of community systems. It complements the Community Principles and Community Guidelines. It is not a complete etiquette guide; ordinary participation expectations are addressed in the Guidelines.

This policy applies to conduct using storykōbō and to off-platform conduct where there is a credible, material connection to a member's safety or participation on the service.

## 2. Prohibited conduct

Members must not use storykōbō to:

- Threaten, stalk, intimidate, coerce, exploit, or target another person; encourage or coordinate real-world violence; or organize discriminatory harassment.
- Promote or recruit for violent extremist activity, or use community spaces for extremist propaganda, intimidation, or recruitment.
- Run scams; impersonate another person or organization; knowingly misrepresent identity, authority, affiliation, or intent to obtain access, money, information, or influence.
- Conduct abusive, coercive, or deceptive group recruitment, including recruitment that conceals material purpose or pressures members to participate.
- Send spam, conduct unsolicited mass promotion, manipulate engagement, or disrupt community spaces through repetitive or automated activity.
- Scrape, harvest, bulk-copy, or collect member information or creative work without authorization, including for datasets, model training, or commercial reuse.
- Access accounts, data, systems, or restricted areas without authorization; bypass access controls; exploit vulnerabilities; interfere with service availability; or knowingly introduce malicious code.
- Evade a moderation restriction, suspension, or removal through another account or identity.
- Fabricate evidence or reports, coordinate knowingly false complaints, retaliate against reporters or appeal participants, or manipulate reporting and review systems.
- Exploit another member's account, personal information, creative work, vulnerability, or trust.
- Promote dangerous real-world challenges or instructions likely to cause serious harm.
- Use community tools, events, or features in a way that materially undermines their intended purpose or the safety of participants.

## 3. Accounts and identity

Multiple accounts, pseudonyms, pen names, and separate creative identities are permitted. Their existence alone is not evidence of abuse. The concern is deceptive or harmful use, including evading a restriction, impersonating someone, manipulating community systems, or concealing coordinated misconduct.

Shared devices, households, networks, or locations are not by themselves proof that accounts belong to the same person or are acting together. Decisions should rely on credible evidence and context.

## 4. Security boundaries and authorized testing

Members may not test, probe, access, or alter systems beyond the permissions they have been given. Security research or testing requires prior authorization and must remain within the agreed scope. Suspected vulnerabilities should be reported through the designated confidential security-reporting route when available, rather than exploited or publicly exposed.

This member-facing rule does not replace the internal security program or vulnerability-response process.

## 5. Creative, analytical, and educational context

Context matters. Discussion, criticism, historical analysis, journalism, education, satire, and fictional depictions are not automatically endorsements or prohibited advocacy. The relevant question is how the material is being used and whether it crosses the boundaries in this policy or another applicable policy.

Fictional depiction does not create a blanket exception for targeted harassment, threats, recruitment, exploitation, or instructions intended to facilitate real-world harm. Content-specific requirements are addressed in the Creative Work, Copyright & Content Policy.

## 6. Reporting and review integrity

Reports should describe a member's own experience or conduct they directly observed, and should be as accurate as the reporter can make them. A report or appeal made in good faith is protected even when it cannot be substantiated. A mistaken report is not automatically a false report.

Knowingly fabricated reports, coordinated manipulation, retaliation, or misuse of review processes may themselves be violations. Reports are handled under the Safety, Blocking & Moderation Policy, with confidentiality and independent review where feasible.

## 7. Coordinated handling and consequences

One incident may involve several policy categories. Where appropriate, those categories may be recorded and assessed within one coordinated case rather than creating duplicate investigations or penalties. Distinct incidents or distinct harms may justify separate findings or actions.

Responses are context-sensitive and proportionate. They may include education, content or feature restrictions, warnings, temporary suspension, or account removal. Urgent protective action may be taken when needed, with documentation and secondary review when feasible. Notice and appeal are governed by the moderation policy, subject to safety, privacy, and legal constraints.

## 8. No private-contract enforcement

storykōbō governs participation in its community. It does not negotiate, guarantee, administer, or enforce private collaborations, commissions, licenses, publishing arrangements, or other agreements between members. The platform may address harassment, fraud, impersonation, or other misconduct occurring through community interactions without becoming a party to members' private agreements.

## 9. Related policies

Read this policy with the Community Principles, Community Guidelines, Safety, Blocking & Moderation Policy, Privacy Policy, Creative Work, Copyright & Content Policy, and AI Use & Human Authorship Policy.

## 10. Changes and readiness

This is a working policy and may be revised as the service and its capabilities develop. The effective date will be set before launch. Features, reporting routes, technical controls, and review procedures described here must be operationally supported before they are represented as available. The plain-language companion is explanatory; this policy governs if the two differ.
